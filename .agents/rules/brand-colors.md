# SERK Technologies Group Brand & UI Guidelines

Whenever applying company branding or adding new elements:
- **Base UI Theme:** Black (`#101513`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Signature Orange Topic:** Brand Orange / Warm Amber (`#E3AB72`).
- **Inner Pages Header:** Inner pages feature the signature orange animated header banner with typing cursor. Homepage hero starts directly with the headline (no orange topic banner above it).
- **Action Buttons:** Deep Forest Green (`#0D4634`, hover: `#2D735A`).
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#DEE6E0`.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #DEE6E0` border and `8px` rounded corners.
- **Mobile Stack:** Must always be a clean 1-column stack with zero overlapping elements.
