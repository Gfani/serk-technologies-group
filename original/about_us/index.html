About Us |

We Simplify

Investing & Doing Business in Ethiopia

Zuri Capital is a leading financial advisory firm in Ethiopia, dedicated to delivering world-class corporate finance, transaction and capital market advisory services. As Ethiopia undergoes significant economic and financial sector reforms, Zuri Capital is uniquely positioned to provide expert guidance to businesses navigating this evolving landscape.

2022

Establishment and Foundation

Established in Ethiopia with a mission to provide high-quality investment and corporate finance advisory services. The firm begins supporting SMEs and growing enterprises across Ethiopia’s evolving financial landscape.

2023 & 2024

Growth & Market Engagement

Delivered several successful advisory projects across key sectors, while building strong relationships with banks, MFIs, and donor-funded programs. Our team supported companies in raising over ETB 3.5B in debt and equity financing, earning clients' trust in navigating financial structuring and capital sourcing.

2025

Rebranding and Market Expansion

Began rebranding to reflect our expanded focus in Ethiopia’s evolving financial sector. We enhanced our service offering to include IPO readiness, securities advisory, and capital markets compliance, positioning ourselves as a forward-thinking partner for businesses navigating the opportunities and regulations of Ethiopia’s emerging capital market.

Mulugeta Tibebu

Chairman

He currently serves as ESG Manager at Zoscales Partners, supporting portfolio management and fund operations. Paul Reynolds

Director
Paul has over 35 years of experience advising governments, corporates, investors, and banks on M&A, financing, restructuring, and governance. He built and led four successful investment banking platforms for Cazenove and Rothschild across EMEA and India, and has served as lead adviser on 175 announced transactions totaling €250 billion.
